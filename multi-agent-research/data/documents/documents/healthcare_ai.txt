Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems that can perform
tasks that normally require human intelligence.

AI has several applications in healthcare.

AI can assist with medical diagnosis by analyzing patient information
and identifying patterns that may be useful to healthcare professionals.

AI can be used in medical imaging to analyze images such as X-rays,
CT scans, and MRI scans and help identify possible abnormalities.

AI can support personalized medicine by analyzing patient data and
helping healthcare professionals consider treatment options based on
individual patient characteristics.

AI can support clinical decision-making by providing relevant
information and predictions from available healthcare data.

AI can also be used in healthcare robotics and assistive technologies
to support certain medical procedures and patient care activities.

AI in healthcare also has challenges. These include data privacy,
data quality, bias, security, and the need for appropriate human
oversight.

AI systems should support healthcare professionals rather than
automatically replace human medical judgment.
